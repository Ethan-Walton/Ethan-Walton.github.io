My path into computing started in 4th grade and never stopped. From LEGO Robotics in elementary school to TSA competitions in middle and high school, I built a foundation in structured problem-solving long before I wrote my first line of C. What I study & build

My coursework spans low-level systems programming, wireless & mobile computing, data structures, and algorithms. Outside the classroom I apply those concepts directly — writing hex packets for security labs, building C tooling for Linux environments, deploying local AI inference, and co-founding Proactive Survival as co-owner and lead backend developer.

I prefer to understand a system from the ground up before building abstractions on top of it. Whether it's a routing algorithm, a data structure, or a business process, I break it into components, identify constraints, and build something that holds up under real conditions — not just in a demo.
